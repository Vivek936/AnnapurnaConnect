How It Works

Simple. Smart. Dignified.

Three steps to transform surplus food into dignity and nourishment for families across India.

1
🍲

List Surplus Food

Restaurants, event organizers, and households list leftover food with details — quantity, type, location, and best-before time. Set a price: free or nominal (₹1–₹10).

⏱️ Takes under 2 minutes
2
📍

Smart Location Match

Our algorithm instantly matches your listing to verified receivers and volunteers within a 5 km radius — prioritizing urgency, quantity, and dietary preferences.

⚡ Real-time matching
3
🤝

Dignified Distribution

Receivers claim and pick up food with dignity — paying a small nominal price (e.g., ₹1 per roti) that preserves self-respect. Volunteers assist delivery for those unable to travel.

💛 Dignity preserved
