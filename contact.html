❓ Quick FAQs

Is there a registration fee to join? +
No. Joining Annapurna Connect as a donor, receiver, or volunteer is completely free. We are an 80G registered nonprofit.
How is food safety ensured? +
All donors agree to FSSAI food safety guidelines. We also have a community rating system where receivers rate each listing after pickup.
Can I donate food anonymously? +
Yes. Donors can choose to be listed anonymously on the platform, though basic verification is always required for safety.
How do I get 80G tax exemption? +
For monetary CSR contributions, 80G receipts are issued automatically. For food donations, we provide valuation certificates based on market value.
